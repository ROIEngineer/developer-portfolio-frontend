About Me

I never set out to become a developer—it kind of found me. I got curious about AI and machine learning, started tinkering, and before I knew it, I was hooked on building things with code.

What started as curiosity turned into something bigger: a way to create solutions, solve problems, and honestly, build a better life for my family. I chose web development because it's practical—you can see what you build, people can use it, and the skills are immediately applicable to real-world needs.

The journey hasn't been easy. I've spent countless late nights debugging, countless tutorials rewatched, and countless "aha!" moments that made it all worth it. What I love most about development isn't just writing code—it's the problem-solving, the creativity, and knowing that what I build can actually make someone's day easier.

Right now, I'm focused on mastering full-stack JavaScript development and building projects that showcase not just technical skills, but thoughtful design and user experience. I'm looking for opportunities to grow, contribute to meaningful projects, and continue learning every single day.
